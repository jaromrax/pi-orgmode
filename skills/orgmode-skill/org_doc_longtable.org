* Some Latex stuff

Tricks to put correct latex :
 - ~ tilde AS A non-breaking space in Latex may be simulated by: At 40\nbsp{}MeV
 - micrometer - greek symbol - may be simulated as:  thickness 25\nbsp{}\mu{}m
   - NOT WITH the dolar sign -  25 $\mu$m
   - NOT just separated mu and m - 26 \mu m
 - upper-index with \null before: \null^{40}Ca ions
